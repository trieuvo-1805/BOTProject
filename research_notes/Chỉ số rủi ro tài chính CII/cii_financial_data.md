# CII (HOSE: CII) – Consolidated financial data 2019 – Q3/2026 for financial-risk ratios

Units: VND billion (tỷ đồng) unless stated. All figures are consolidated (hợp nhất) unless flagged. Research date: 15/11/2026.

**Source note.** The main multi-year source is StockAnalysis.com (S&P-style standardized data, reported in VND million and rounded here). I reconciled it against Vietnamese press that quotes the audited consolidated statements:
- **Profit lines reconcile once you read the labels correctly.** StockAnalysis "Net Income" + "Minority Interest in Earnings" = consolidated PAT reported by VietTimes: 2025 124+244=368 vs 368; 2024 260+361=621 vs 618; 2023 178+192=370 vs 370; 2022 695+165=860 vs 861; 2021 -332+90=-242 vs -242. So **StockAnalysis "Net Income" ≈ profit attributable to the parent (LNST cổ đông công ty mẹ)**.
- **Balance-sheet totals reconcile.** Total assets 2025 = 37,525 and liabilities = 25,568 in both sources.
- **StockAnalysis "Shareholders' Equity" includes minority interest.**
- **StockAnalysis "Total Debt" includes convertible bonds and some other items, so it is greater than ST debt + LT debt.**

## 1. Balance sheet (key items) 2019 – Q3/2026

### Takeaway
Total assets grew from ~29.2k (2019) to ~44.9k (30/9/2026). Interest-bearing debt rose from ~17.0k (2021) to ~28.8k (Q3/2026), most of it after mid-2025: about +7k in 9M/2026, driven by the Trung Lương – Mỹ Thuận (TL–MT) expansion, PC1 share purchases and term deposits. Equity incl. minority rose to ~13.2k after convertible-bond conversions. The asset base is dominated by BOT toll-collection rights (~15k, booked as intangible assets) and real-estate work in progress (~4.5–5.1k).

## 2. Income statement 2019 – Q3/2026

### Takeaway
- **Revenue and margins.** Revenue has been flat at ~3.0k since 2023. BOT toll revenue (~2.8k in 2025) now makes up ~85–95% of revenue and keeps gross margins at ~55–57%.
- **Interest burden.** Interest expense (~1.3–1.4k a year) consumes almost all operating profit. Profit depends on financial income and one-off gains, such as divestments and NBB revaluation.
- **Profit trend.** PAT fell from 861 (2022) to 368 (2025). Profit attributable to the parent was only ~124 in 2025 and ~39 in 9M/2026.

## 3. Cash flow statement

### Takeaway
- **CFO is weak and volatile.** It was negative in 2021, 2023, 2025 and 9M/2026, at −1,651 in 9M/2026.
- **Interest burden.** Cash interest paid (~1.5–2.1k a year) exceeds EBITDA-based cash generation.
- **Funding gap.** Since 2023 the gap has been filled by net new borrowing. In 2026 it widened sharply because of TL–MT capex and investments: 9M/2026 CFI was −5.7k and CFF +7.1k.
